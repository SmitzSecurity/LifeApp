# Recurring budget workflow — September 16, 2026

This update adds weekly, every-two-week and bounded custom schedules alongside the existing monthly and annual schedules. Custom schedules cover selected weekdays every N weeks, selected dates or ordinal weekdays every N months, and a month/day every N years. Advanced schedules require a start date to anchor the interval. Start/end bounds are inclusive; installments count scheduled occurrences, not confirmations. Coincident rules produce one occurrence. Dates beyond a month’s length use its final day.

Legacy monthly/annual confirmations keep `due:YYYY-MM:recurring-id`. Advanced schedules use `due:YYYY-MM-DD:recurring-id` and an immutable `occurrenceDate` separate from the actual payment date. Each occurrence is confirmed independently. Actual dates remain in the original budget month. Income allocation IDs inherit their source’s date-specific identity. Purge suppression, versioned exact retries and portable exports must support both ID shapes.

Credit-card reminders use `kind: transfer`. They have no expense category, income policy or loan principal projection. A zero expected amount means the next statement amount is unknown; confirmation requires a positive actual amount. Optional `paymentDueDay` distinguishes the creditor deadline from a monthly payment date. Card settlements do not increase spending or savings or reduce the recorded cash-flow metric again. This app tracks confirmations; it never changes creditor autopay settings or moves money.

The AI review starts every expense with an unassigned category, including previously generated drafts. Users choose an existing category or create one in the review. Suggested category allowances are optional and require explicit application; model-generated names never silently create categories or replace allowances. Unknown card amounts and unsupported/ambiguous source details remain visible for review. Provider schema configuration stays omitted for Budget, with the full JSON shape in instructions and strict local validation.

Paid expected rows offer an explicit undo confirmation. Recurring payments move to the existing seven-day Trash and become expected again; confirmed one-off plans return to planned status. Independently confirmed savings/investment transfers are not reversed when income is undone. Deleted/voided payments do not lock the recurring item’s category, while active occurrences still do. Historical snapshots, stale-write checks and Trash restoration remain protected.

Schedule edits must preserve the occurrence identities of active recorded payments, including inside the atomic save. Monthly date edits retain their original month identity; advanced schedules can change only while keeping already-paid dates. Pausing or deleting a recurrence does not rewrite its historical payments. A permanently deleted or expired occurrence is excluded from future forecasts and AI context; its stable ID remains retired. To record that payment again after permanent deletion, create a fresh one-off transaction.

Forecast labels inside Expected income & expenses are Upcoming Income and Upcoming Expenses. Card-payment reminders have their own transfer total. Other recurring items retains schedules that do not occur in the selected month.

Annual budgeting: a monthly saving target of annual cost / 12 smooths funding, while the actual annual expense is recorded once when paid. The app retains the full renewal charge in its due month; it does not create twelve additional expenses. Reference: [FDIC Money Smart spending and saving conversion table](https://catalog.fdic.gov/catalog/sfc/servlet.shepherd/document/download/069t000000Bch09AAB).

The first annual expense save/import offers an optional Annual bills sinking fund. The account-wide `profile.annualFund` setting remembers acceptance or decline across months; its dedicated save compares only this setting and merges with the latest profile version. Enabling includes existing and subsequent active annual expenses unless the user marks `excludeFromAnnualFund`. Expired schedules are excluded from the target. The combined annual estimate is divided by twelve and rounded up once to cents. This target is not a bank transfer or a recorded monthly expense.

Confirmed savings transfers tagged `annualFund: contribution` increase the fund’s tracked balance; annual bills explicitly marked `annualFund: payment` reduce it. Pending, voided and deleted transactions do not affect the balance. The balance spans months up through the selected budget month and refreshes on corrections/undo. Disabling the fund preserves its transaction history and permits corrections to existing tagged records. New allocations need enabled settings; regular savings, card repayments and untagged annual bills are never silently assigned to the fund. These transactions retain the usual history, edits, Trash and export behavior.

No database migration or live financial-data rewrite is needed. Keep migrations 0000–0010, Cron, consents and owner/default usage safeguards unchanged. After advanced schedules or transfer records are saved, use forward fixes rather than rollback to older code that rejects or misinterprets them. Monthly loan-payoff mathematics remains restricted to monthly schedules.
