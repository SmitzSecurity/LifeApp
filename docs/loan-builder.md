# Loan presets and assisted import — September 16, 2026

Loans & payoff offers manual presets and Build with AI. Presets cover mortgage, credit card, student loan, car loan, personal loan, medical payment plan and other debt. They choose editable calculation hints, not a lender rate, payment, balance or repayment date. Users can track a statement balance before a payment schedule is known.

Loan data remains optional `debt` metadata on the existing recurring item. Added optional fields are `loanType`, `paymentStatus`, `accruedInterestCents` and `interestAccrual`; original principal and annual rate can be unknown. Existing saved loans retain their prior defaults. A balance-only loan does not create expected payments and cannot receive a first recurring-payment confirmation until a schedule is supplied. Principal and existing uncapitalized interest stay separate. Missing original principal does not produce a fictional progress percentage.

The estimate uses a dated principal/interest baseline. Known daily/monthly accrual may be estimated; paused accrual does not add interest. Unknown accrual or rate, and statement-only methods, show the recorded statement balance without inventing payoff. Credit-card debt uses the transfer kind and statement tracking: confirming a payment does not count purchases again as spending, and new purchases, multiple APRs and lender-specific interest charges require an updated statement. Monthly debt schedules remain the supported payoff scope.

Loan AI is an optional `intent: loans` on the existing Budget build request and snapshot. It shares the `budget:` namespace, all admission/count/spending limits, archived usage, reservations and unknown-outcome blockers. Intent is part of exact retry identity. Loan drafts are listed separately in the UI, while blockers are checked across both modes. No new provider, credentials, runtime flags or database tables are introduced.

The builder accepts pasted text, JPEG/PNG/WebP uploads or clipboard images, PDF, TXT/MD/CSV/TSV/JSON, XLSX and DOCX through the existing bounded extraction path. The explicit Build action sends the selected input to Gemini. Text is retained with the draft; image/PDF bytes are fingerprinted rather than stored. File size limits, Office archive bounds, token preflight and provider output validation remain intact. Unsupported or encrypted files produce actionable errors.

AI must separate loan groups with different rates and keep the dates attached to their evidence. It must not combine an aggregate with its component loans, infer zero interest from missing data, invent payment dates, mix principal and interest from incompatible dates, or reinterpret deferred-interest offers as unconditional zero-interest loans. Unknown financial terms remain unknown. A balance-only draft can be reviewed without fabricating a schedule. Only selected, reviewed loans are adopted; expense categories still require explicit assignment or inline creation. Adoption creates no actual payments.

Presets are informed by primary explanations of [mortgage principal and payment components](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/on-a-mortgage-whats-the-difference-between-my-principal-and-interest-payment-and-my-total-monthly-payment-en-1941/), [simple versus precomputed auto-loan interest](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/whats-the-difference-between-a-simple-interest-rate-and-precomputed-interest-on-an-auto-loan-en-841/), [daily student-loan interest](https://mohela.studentaid.gov/DL/resourceCenter/StudentLoanInterest.aspx), and [credit-card balances with different APRs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/my-bill-shows-different-aprs-and-shows-how-much-of-the-balance-is-subject-to-each-interest-rate-i-cannot-figure-out-how-this-is-calculated-how-does-that-work-en-39/). Defaults are editable and do not establish a particular lender's contract.

No migration or live-data rewrite is required. Keep migrations 0000–0010, Cron, automatic-analysis/email consents, private owner allowances and unknown holds unchanged. Saved new fields require compatible forward fixes rather than rollback to older strict schemas.
