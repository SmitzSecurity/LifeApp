## Temporary owner allowance

The operator can configure two private Worker runtime bindings: `LIFEAPP_AI_OWNER_USER_ID` (an existing opaque `google:` account ID) and `LIFEAPP_AI_OWNER_LIMITS_UNTIL` (an ISO UTC timestamp ending in `.000Z`). Resolve the ID against the existing verified Google account; never identify the owner by a client-supplied email or role, embed their email/ID in source, or link/merge accounts. Missing, malformed, non-Google or expired configuration grants no override.

September 16, 2026: the owner authorized a development allowance of $1 per UTC day. Until expiry, only that account has a $1 daily spending ceiling, 25 total AI attempts per UTC day, 10 attempts per builder purpose per UTC day, and five regenerations per analysis period per UTC day. Its monthly ceiling is $1 times the number of days in the current UTC month ($30 in September, $31 in October); the daily ceiling still applies independently. Every admission includes the new $0.20 reservation plus measured charges and unresolved reservations already attributed to that UTC day. Thus an attempt can be refused with less than $1 measured when less than $0.20 remains available. Unspent daily headroom does not roll over.

Everyone else retains 5 total attempts per day, 2 per builder, 2 regenerations and $1 per account per month. While the private owner override is valid, the ordinary shared $5 monthly pool excludes only that opaque owner account. A second aggregate monthly ceiling includes the entire ledger and cannot exceed the ordinary $5 plus the owner's monthly ceiling. This prevents development testing from consuming the ordinary pool without creating an unlimited exception. Both atomic admission paths use the same spending predicate; automatic and manual analyses share it with routine, budget and training builds. Deleted/archived usage still counts in the appropriate pool, unknown outcomes retain holds, duplicate requests do not run again, and the cost circuit breaker is unchanged. This grants no automatic-analysis or email consent and purchases no provider credits.

The initial private override expires October 14, 2026 at 23:59:59 UTC. At expiry it falls back to standard limits without clearing any usage; the ordinary shared $5 pool once again counts every account, including all earlier owner usage. An account or shared pool already above its normal allowance waits for the relevant reset. Removing either binding disables the override immediately. Keep these runtime values out of generated configuration and public Git history. No new binding or migration is needed for the daily spending policy.
