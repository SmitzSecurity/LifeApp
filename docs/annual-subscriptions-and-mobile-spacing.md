# Annual subscriptions and mobile spacing

September 15, 2026. Recurring items now support `frequency: "annual"` with a required `month` from 1 to 12 and the existing `day`. Monthly payloads retain their defaults and do not acquire an extra month value. Annual amounts are full charges, never divided into monthly actuals. Estimates appear in the matching renewal month only, in both category allowances and expected payments; confirming them uses the existing month/recurring occurrence ID and actual-payment flow. Off-month new payments are rejected; corrections and exact retries for existing actuals remain valid.

Start/end dates are inclusive. Installment counts count scheduled occurrences, not confirmed payments: an annual count advances yearly from the first eligible renewal. A missing day, such as February 29 in a non-leap year, uses that month's final day, matching existing monthly scheduling. A future unsaved monthly plan inherits annual items through the existing carry-forward behavior; this does not rewrite previously saved plans. An open actual-payment draft remains mounted if its recurrence is moved to another renewal month, but authoritative totals and server eligibility follow the saved schedule.

AI budget extraction can return explicit annual subscriptions/income with their full amounts and known renewal months. It must omit ambiguous missing months and unsupported frequencies with a note, rather than fabricate monthly payments. Imported drafts still need review and separate Save; previous monthly drafts remain readable. Loan payoff tracking continues to require monthly payments. Annual ordinary expenses are supported; annual debt tracking is explicitly rejected so monthly interest math is never misapplied.

No migration or production data rewrite is required. All completed migrations 0000–0010 remain intact. After an annual item or draft is saved, do not roll back to a schema that rejects annual frequency. Preserve usage/consents, Trash accounting and all existing exact retries.

Mobile changes use the existing theme colors: lighter labels, a compact growing journal, two equal Cancel/Save areas pinned at the bottom, and one compact workout overview combining weekly totals and training analysis. Workout logging actions share a row and program Start remains prominent. Program fields scroll separately from their action footer. The microphone remains inside the journal at bottom right with the existing speech glow, and cancellation stops voice and drops late callbacks. Touch targets remain at least 44px for changed primary controls; browser zoom stays available. The confirmed 64px navigation/footer fix is retained.

Use `node scripts/browser-smoke.mjs --calm --editing --analysis --history --dictation --light` for a disposable light-theme fixture (omit `--light` for OLED). Check renewal/nonrenewal forecasts, annual save/reopen, draft preservation after renewal changes, independent scrolling/pinned actions, journal growth/voice cancellation, narrow/mobile/desktop layouts, and existing retry behavior. Synthetic fixture controls can cover lower-right buttons; use keyboard Enter for those actions. No live paid generation, journal write or email is needed.
