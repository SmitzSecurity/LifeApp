LifeApp retains only geometry from SVG assets and decodes original PNGs into clean raster images. Raster motion frames use verified translation offsets against stationary apparatus, at their original scale, followed by one shared padded crop. Static Workout Guide illustrations are opaque monochrome masks with cropped, padded viewBoxes. Original Everkinetic start/end pairs preserve their colors and share one padded viewBox to keep playback aligned.
