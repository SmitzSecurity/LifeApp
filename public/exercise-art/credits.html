LifeApp retains only geometry from SVG assets and decodes original PNGs into clean raster images. Raster motion frames use verified translation offsets against stationary apparatus, at their original scale, followed by one shared padded crop. Static Workout Guide illustrations use opaque monochrome masks and padded cropped viewBoxes. Original Everkinetic start/end drawings retain their original colors and use one shared padded viewBox for aligned playback. Each record includes the exact changes and original source.
